# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Four presets measured on a Steam Deck OLED: Battery (30 fps), Balanced (45 fps), Max FPS
  (60 fps) and Max Quality (30 fps, everything high).
- Keep / Revert dialog after every apply; your own settings are saved first and can be restored.
- Customize any preset setting; per-setting and per-preset reset.
- Steam Deck tab with the Quick Access settings for each preset.
- Controller navigation for the window, dialogs and minimap menu.
- UI scale for the 7-inch screen.
- Raid and battleground settings follow the presets.
- Optional relog reminder when the frame rate sags over a long session.
